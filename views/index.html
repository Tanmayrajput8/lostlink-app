How LostLink Works

Simple, reliable, and admin-verified lost and found for your campus

1. Submit a Report

Lost your ID, keys, or backpack? Found a wallet near the library? Submit a detailed report with a photo and location.

2. Admin Review & Approval

The administrator reviews submitted reports and approves them to make them visible in the public catalog.

3. Claim & Return

Owners identify their found items, submit ownership proof, and the administrator verifies before arranging the handover.
